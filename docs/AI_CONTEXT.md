# AI-Based Medicine Reminder & Health Tracking System

## Project Goal

Build a healthcare support system that helps patients manage
medicine schedules, track health parameters, and notify caregivers
about missed medications or configured abnormal health readings.

1. Authentication

5. Medication Tracking
6. Health Tracking
7. Caregiver Alerts
8. AI Health Summary

## Safety Rules

The system is a healthcare support system.

AI must NOT:
- Diagnose diseases
- Prescribe medicines
- Change medication dosage
- Replace doctors

AI may:
- Summarize recorded information
- Identify trends for review
- Generate general supportive suggestions

Health alerts should use predefined/configurable rules.
